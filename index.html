Is the training suitable for beginners?+

The school's promotion is aimed at people learning car driving. Confirm the exact beginner training process directly with the school.

How long is the program?+

The advertised proposition is a 15-day car driving training program. Ask the school about the exact schedule and daily duration.

Do you teach hill and clutch control?+

Hill clutch and car control are specifically mentioned in the school's promotional material.

Can I use my own car during training?+

Yes! If you have your own car, we switch to it from day 11. Otherwise, you can use our school car for the full 15 days.

What if I need practice after training?+

We offer 1–2 days per week of supervised practice even after your course ends — so you stay confident.
